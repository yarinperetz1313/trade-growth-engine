# Pilot Zero Business-First Presentation

## Outcome

- Problem: the supported Pilot Zero journey is functionally coherent but its
  ordinary first-run presentation leads with import/audit mechanics, lets
  sample and instrumentation compete with customer truth, and makes the
  human-controlled action lifecycle feel like a separate technical workflow.
- Intended observable result: a small or medium B2B operator can read the
  product thesis immediately and follow existing opportunity data → trustworthy
  operational readiness → strongest imported-customer economic problem → why
  now → reviewed, approved internal task without any false customer-result or
  autonomous-action claim.
- Explicit non-goals: API/backend/persistence/schema/migration/detector/tenant/
  RLS/case-ordering/monetary-calculation/RevenueAction-authority changes;
  connectors; AI ranking; autonomous outbound; attribution, ROI, or recovered-
  revenue claims; additional detectors; generic CRM/BI; provider/deployment;
  and fake customer evidence.

## Bounded implementation plan

1. Characterize the current import, mapping, readiness, queue grouping,
   feedback, Pilot instrumentation, deep-link, and RevenueAction lifecycle
   composition. Add the smallest RED contracts for business-first desktop and
   390px hierarchy plus sample/customer and result-truth boundaries.
2. Recompose intake around one clear opportunity-import CTA and supported-data
   guidance. Lead mapping with required fields, blocking issues, and business
   impact while retaining exact evidence behind labelled disclosure.
3. Put Operational Data Health before the explicit scan in a calm three-step
   journey. Present the strongest server-ordered imported-customer case first;
   keep sample/demo and operator diagnostics separate without re-ranking either
   disclosed group.
4. Make case → RevenueAction continuous through existing deep links and
   mutations. Carry business value/why context, show Review → Approve → Create
   internal task, and end with a truthful task-created panel that explicitly
   says no message was sent and makes no revenue/ROI claim.
5. Improve desktop and 390px ordinary success, loading, reconciling, empty, and
   error hierarchy. Run progressive focused/affected verification, then one
   near-candidate integration/managed-Chromium/build/harness/migration/diff/
   artifact gate. Capture uncommitted after screenshots and label automated
   timing as internal deterministic rehearsal only.
